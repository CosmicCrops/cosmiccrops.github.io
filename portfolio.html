John Denk — AI Automation Portfolio

AI Automation & Operations Lead — Prestige Path Consulting LLC (Aug 2025 – Present)

Agentic workflow design, human-in-the-loop review, and documented results across three operating businesses.

1. Multi-business content orchestration (Prestige Path · Cosmic Crops · PowerScope)

Problem: Four social accounts across three brands needed consistent posting with no staff time to run them.

Built: Scheduled AI agent pipelines that research, draft, design, and publish content on fixed cadences (Instagram, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest), each with brand-specific guardrails — verified figures only, no personal contact details in public posts, and a “single-out” privacy test that blocks any story matching exactly one real client.

Result: Posting runs unattended multiple times per week across all brands; failures (expired approvals, generator rejections) are detected, reported, and re-queued without duplicate posts.

2. Verified job-lead pipeline & participant newsletter

Problem: Job seekers on disability benefits were being sent stale or expired job postings from aggregators.

Built: A verification workflow with a hard standard — posting date within 14 days on the posting page itself, full-page scan for expired/closed language, employer-owned career pages preferred over aggregators, and a fresh same-day link re-check before any audience send. Verified leads are assembled into one regional newsletter (Mailchimp) sorted by participant area.

Result: A 445-employer verified hiring-contacts database supports the pipeline; the Nov 2, 2026 edition delivered 13 of 13 with 0 bounces and a 38.5% open rate at first check, and click data is used to tighten later editions to fewer, better-matched leads.

3. Client job-search assistant with escalation boundaries

Problem: Client inquiries arrived around the clock; benefits questions require a licensed professional’s judgment and must never be answered by automation.

Built: An agent that monitors the business inbox every 15 minutes and handles job-search help autonomously (openings, resumes, applications) inside a strict lane: job leads only. Anything touching benefits is flagged to the owner personally and never answered by the agent.

Result: Clients get same-day job-search help at all hours, while regulated advice stays 100% human — a human-review/escalation design pattern transferable to any compliance-sensitive workflow.

4. AI-assisted publishing pipeline (participant textbook + 21-book catalog)

Problem: A plain-language benefits guide for SSI/SSDI recipients had to be accurate enough to trust with people’s benefits — and produced by a one-person business.

Built: A research-and-production pipeline that synthesized 1,181 catalogued source documents, audited every dollar figure against SSA’s published amounts, and ran render-level QA on every rebuild before delivery. The same workflow maintains three live business websites and a 21-title book catalog site.

Result: “Working With Your Benefits” (2027 Edition) — a 40,000+ word textbook (237-page free participant PDF, live Nov 1, 2026, and delivered to participants in the Nov 2 newsletter) with Kindle and print editions staged for KDP, produced and QA’d end-to-end with AI agents under owner review at every gate.

5. Partner-contact research database (Partnership Plus)

Problem: A national referral outreach to state vocational rehabilitation agencies needed real, current program contacts — not scraped guesses.

Built: A verified-contact research workflow: published contacts only, each dated and sourced to an official agency page, gaps tracked explicitly until filled.

Result: 34 verified contacts across 30 states (program, provider-relations, and business-services roles), ready for owner-approved outreach.

6. Conversational AI assistant design (Prestige Path client assistant + public engagement)

Problem: People asking about jobs and benefits on Instagram and Threads needed fast, helpful answers — but benefits advice is regulated, personal data must never be singled out or exposed, and a wrong automated answer can harm a real person.

Designed: The conversation system end-to-end: intents and lanes (job-search help answered autonomously; benefits questions never answered by the agent and escalated to the owner personally), tone and length rules (concise, useful-first, no hard sell), privacy guardrails (a “single-out” test that blocks any message whose details could identify one client; no personal phone or client details in public replies — DM is the contact route), and failure handling (one reply per thread per check cycle, per-thread daily caps, no duplicate sends after a failed session until the thread is re-read).

Result: The assistant handles job-search conversations around the clock across Instagram DMs and Threads under those rules, with every benefits question reaching the human owner unanswered by automation — a working example of conversational design where the escalation and refusal paths are the product, not an afterthought.
